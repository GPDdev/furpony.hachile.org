ABOUT THE PROJECT

From within communities.
Open to questions.

Furpony Institute is an independent writing and discussion project exploring similarities, differences, and relationships between Furry and Brony cultures. We welcome personal observations, cultural comparisons, creative journals, interviews, and responses. Lived experience can start a conversation; different views can keep it going.
